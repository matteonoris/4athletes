This TestFlight build makes health data more reliable and expands team analytics.

- Automatically refreshes health data when the app returns to the foreground without repeated permission prompts.
- Preserves valid recovery and strain scores during partial refreshes or network failures.
- Improves Health workout importing and deduplication and loads the complete body-metrics history.
- Adds team leaderboards with period filters and metrics for hours, skiing, strength, running, jumps, and speed.
- Fixes attendance percentages and team averages, including absences and athletes without activity.
- Improves team-report layout, readability, and details in light and dark themes.
