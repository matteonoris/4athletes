This update restores team visibility with the protected team access system and adds new workout tools.

- Restores existing teams, member lists, invitations, attendance and team reports in the updated app.
- Adds manager controls for coach approval and team membership, with personal health measurements read-only for coaches.
- Adds health-data consent and revocation controls, clearer Apple Health access guidance and account deletion from the profile.
- Imports exercises from photos and PDFs on the device, with review before adding them to a workout and matching to the exercise catalog.
- Adds workout place suggestions and map previews, improves athlete reports and activity details, and preserves the dedicated alpine-skiing workflow.
