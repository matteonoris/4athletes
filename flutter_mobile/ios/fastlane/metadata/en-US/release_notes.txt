This TestFlight build introduces instant athletic test evaluation in Analytics.

- Colors test cards red, yellow, or green using the reference benchmarks.
- Automatically applies the correct thresholds for sex and age group, including SENIOR athletes over 21.
- Evaluates jumping, speed, aerobic capacity, balance, core, and strength while preserving the Analytics layout.
- Requires an explicit sex selection during registration.
- Fixes completion and persistence of Google or Apple profiles when sex is still missing.
