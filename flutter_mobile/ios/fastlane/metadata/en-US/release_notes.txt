This TestFlight build fixes missing training hours in team rankings.

- Reliably saves completed athlete training sessions before leaving the form.
- Correctly credits backdated completed sessions in the selected ranking period.
- Credits present athletes for completed coach athletic training, even if a generated session row is temporarily missing.
- Prevents duplicate credit when both the coach event and athlete session exist.
- Shows save and synchronization errors instead of reporting a false success.
- Adds pull-to-refresh to team rankings.
