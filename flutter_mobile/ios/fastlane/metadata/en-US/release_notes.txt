This update improves Apple Health imports and daily movement tracking.

- Removes unused microphone, menstrual-flow and heartbeat-series access; HRV continues to use Apple Health SDNN.
- Restores the native Apple Health connection for workout details with the current iOS lifecycle and clarifies why health data is read.
- Shows daily steps and steps outside workouts, with a limited contribution to daily strain and clear unavailable-data states.
- Improves navigation from health-score notifications and adds email/password sign-in alongside Google and Apple.
- Preserves health-data consent, revocation and account-deletion controls.
