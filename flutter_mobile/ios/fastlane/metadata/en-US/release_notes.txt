TestFlight build focused on monthly team reporting for coaches.

- Adds a monthly team report with attendance, ski volume, athletic workload, physical test metrics, alerts, and athlete comparisons.
- Adds printable/shareable PDF export, with optional individual athlete sheets.
- Exposes the report from the coach dashboard for authorized coach/admin users.
- Adds calculator coverage for programmed vs out-of-program work, event fallbacks, deltas, alerts, and PDF generation.
- Keeps Hyperarch and tendon isometrics available in sport selection on mobile and the local web prototype.
