TestFlight build focused on Health workout import coverage, sleep insights, workout templates, wearable metric persistence, and dryland session readability.

- Maps more Apple Health workout types, including core training, Pilates, mixed cardio, stairs, martial arts, and recovery sessions.
- Keeps Health score cards populated from saved logs when viewing past days or when today's sleep data is still incomplete, with expanded sleep trend charts.
- Syncs a longer wearable metric history and stores SpO2, respiratory rate, temperature, HRV, and weight logs remotely.
- Adds workout template storage support and improves dryland training summaries with expandable strength blocks, set details, badges, volume, max load, and 1RM percentages.
- Refines the seasonal dashboard header layout on narrow screens.
